The fixture carries no prose of its own: each element is labelled with its name
and padded with lorem ipsum, so that layout is judged on neutral text. Some
labels are also addresses the tests reach for, so renaming one means updating
them: `#default-style` (headings), `#heading-1` and `#heading-3` (table of
contents), `#lst-captioned-listing` (figures), and the link text "OpenGraph
Link" (links). The link-style section headings are keys of `STYLES` in
tests/design/links.spec.ts. Credit lines were kept as written, since they are
licence attribution.
