# tests/design/a11y.spec.ts

## 2026-09-16 — axe cannot run in the nojs profile

`@axe-core/playwright` injects axe into the page and runs it there, so under
`javaScriptEnabled: false` it does not fail — it hangs, and the test dies on
the 30s timeout. Two of the suite's 21 original failures were this, and they
cost 60s of every run.

Restricted to `wide` and `narrow`. The `print` profile is excluded on its own
merits: its palette is black on white, so axe's colour rules pass trivially
there, and its other rules describe a screen reader rather than paper.
